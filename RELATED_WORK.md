# Measurement precedent

Chun, Y., Hur, J., & Hwang, J. (2024). AI technology specialization and
national competitiveness. *PLoS ONE*, 19(4), e0301091.
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0301091

Publisher article verified on 16 November 2026:
https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0301091

The paper uses RCA-based country-technology specialization measures and
examines scientific and technological capabilities. It supports the measurement
interpretation of relative specialization, not the claim that the present
distance association has a robust inverted U. Its transition-oriented measures
must be distinguished from next-year RCA status without an initial-state filter.
